# Video Content

## Goal
Build a video content strategy that reaches developers where they learn — YouTube, embedded site demos, and social video. Imgix's URL-based API is inherently visual and demo-friendly.

## Video Types to Create
1. **Product demos** — Show URL-based transformations in real time
2. **Technical tutorials** — "How to optimize images with Imgix and [framework]"
3. **Customer stories** — Video versions of case studies
4. **Feature walkthroughs** — New feature announcements with visual demos
5. **Comparison videos** — "Imgix vs [Competitor]: Speed Test"

## Why This Matters for Imgix
- YouTube is the #2 search engine and frequently cited by Google AI Overviews
- Developers prefer video tutorials for implementation guidance
- Imgix's URL-based transforms are inherently visual — perfect for screen recordings
- Video content gets cited by AI answer engines (especially Google AI Overviews)
