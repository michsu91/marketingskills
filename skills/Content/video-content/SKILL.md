# Video Content

## Goal
Build a video content strategy that reaches developers where they learn — YouTube, embedded site demos, and social video. Imgix's URL-based API is inherently visual, which makes it unusually demo-friendly: you can show a transformation happening in real time just by changing a URL parameter.

## Why Video Matters for Imgix

1. **YouTube is the #2 search engine** and frequently cited by Google AI Overviews. Ranking for "image optimization tutorial" or "responsive images guide" on YouTube extends Imgix's reach beyond web search.
2. **Developers prefer video for implementation.** When choosing between reading docs and watching a 5-minute tutorial, many developers pick the video.
3. **Imgix's URL-based transforms are inherently visual.** Changing `?w=400` to `?w=800` and seeing the image update instantly is a natural screen recording format. Few developer tools demo this well.
4. **Video content gets cited by AI answer engines** (especially Google AI Overviews), which surfaces video results for "how to" queries.
5. **Imgix now has video processing capabilities.** Creating video content about a video product is a natural fit and doubles as product demonstration.

## Video Types to Create

### 1. Product Demos (Highest Priority)
Show Imgix in action: URL-based transformations, dashboard source setup, AI features, video processing.
- **Format:** Screen recording with voiceover (2-5 minutes)
- **Example topics:**
  - "Imgix in 60 seconds: optimize any image with a URL"
  - "Background removal with Imgix AI: no API, no code"
  - "Turn any image into video with Imgix Motion API"
  - "Responsive images with Imgix and Next.js"

### 2. Technical Tutorials (High Priority)
Step-by-step implementation guides for developers.
- **Format:** Screen recording + code editor + browser preview (5-15 minutes)
- **Example topics:**
  - "How to set up Imgix with AWS S3 in 5 minutes"
  - "Building an image gallery with real-time transformations"
  - "Imgix Video: HLS streaming with URL parameters"
  - "Migrate from Cloudinary to Imgix: step by step"

### 3. Feature Walkthroughs
New feature announcements with visual demonstrations. Pair with blog post launches.
- **Format:** Short demo (1-3 minutes) published same day as the launch blog post
- **Coordinates with:** Product-Marketing/launch-strategy for launch timing

### 4. Comparison Videos
"Imgix vs [Competitor]: Speed Test" or "Image quality comparison: Imgix vs Cloudinary."
- **Format:** Side-by-side screen recording showing the same image processed by both tools
- **Be fair and factual.** Developers will call out dishonest comparisons immediately.

### 5. Customer Stories (Video)
Video versions of case studies. Customer talks about their experience and shows their implementation.
- **Format:** Interview or testimonial (3-5 minutes)
- **Coordinates with:** Content/case-studies for sourcing participants

## Video SEO

For every video published:
- **Title:** Keyword-first, benefit-clear. "How to Optimize Images with Imgix and Next.js" not "Imgix Tutorial #4"
- **Description:** 2-3 sentence summary, relevant links (docs, blog post, free trial), timestamps
- **Tags:** Target keywords + related terms
- **Chapters:** Add timestamps for each section
- **Transcript:** Upload or auto-generate (aids both SEO and accessibility)
- **Embed on imgix.com:** Every YouTube video should also be embedded on a relevant page for additional SEO value

## Production Guidelines
- Keep it simple. Screen recordings with clear voiceover. No need for expensive production.
- Show real code and real URLs (from Imgix's demo source or test accounts)
- Include the result of every transformation (before/after or live preview)
- Follow Imgix brand voice for all narration and on-screen text
- Add Imgix branding: simple intro card, consistent outro with CTA

## Posting Cadence
- Start with 1-2 videos per month (realistic for a small team)
- Prioritize product demos and tutorials over polish
- Repurpose: extract 30-second clips for LinkedIn and Twitter
