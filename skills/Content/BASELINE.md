# imgix Content Baseline

**Captured:** April 17, 2026
**Method:** Webflow CMS audit, existing content inventory

---

## Current Content State

### Blog
- Active blog at blog.imgix.com on Webflow CMS
- Mix of product updates, technical tutorials, and customer stories
- Publishing cadence: irregular

### Website Copy
- Homepage, solution pages (ecommerce, media, automotive, real estate, agencies), product pages
- Solution pages exist but are thin on specifics and not keyword-optimized
- Case studies exist (~20+) but not structured for maximum SEO/AEO value

### Social Media
- LinkedIn and Twitter/X presence
- No systematic content repurposing from blog to social

### Presentations
- Imgix brand deck guidelines established with exact colors, typography, and layout rules

### Gaps
1. **No systematic content calendar** — publishing is ad hoc
2. **Case studies not optimized** — exist but lack structured data and SEO optimization
3. **No video content strategy** — no YouTube presence, no demo videos
4. **No developer tutorials** — technical docs exist but not tutorial-style content optimized for search
5. **Social content not systematized** — no repurposing pipeline from long-form to social
