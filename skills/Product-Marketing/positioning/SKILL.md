# Positioning & Messaging Framework

## Goal
Create and maintain Imgix's strategic positioning — the foundational narrative that informs every piece of content, every sales conversation, and every campaign.

## Framework Components
1. **Category** — What market category does Imgix own? ("Visual media platform" vs "image CDN")
2. **Target audience** — Primary and secondary ICPs with specific pain points
3. **Key differentiators** — What makes Imgix uniquely better (not just different)
4. **Value propositions** — By persona, by vertical, by use case
5. **Proof points** — Metrics, customer names, and claims that back up each value prop
6. **Competitive positioning** — How we position against each competitor specifically
7. **Messaging hierarchy** — Primary message → supporting messages → proof points

## Current Positioning Challenge
AI models describe Imgix as a "lightweight specialist" — less feature-rich than Cloudinary. This framing is wrong and needs to change through consistent, authoritative messaging across all touchpoints.
