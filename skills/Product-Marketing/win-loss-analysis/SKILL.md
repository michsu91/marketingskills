# Win-Loss Analysis

## Goal
Understand why Imgix wins and loses deals. Feed insights back into positioning, competitive intel, sales enablement, and product roadmap.

## Analysis Framework
1. **Data collection** — Pull closed deals from HubSpot, review Gong recordings
2. **Pattern identification** — Common reasons for wins/losses, competitor mentions, objections
3. **Segmentation** — By deal size, vertical, competitor, use case
4. **Insight synthesis** — What messaging works, what doesn't, where product gaps exist
5. **Action distribution** — Route insights to relevant systems

## Where Insights Flow
- **Product-Marketing/positioning** — Refine messaging based on what resonates
- **Product-Marketing/competitor-alternatives** — Update battle cards with real objections
- **Product-Marketing/sales-enablement** — New objection handling based on actual losses
- **Conversion/pricing-strategy** — Pricing-related wins/losses inform packaging
