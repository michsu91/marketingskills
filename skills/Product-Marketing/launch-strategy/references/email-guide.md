# Email Guide for Product Launches

Imgix launch emails are short, scannable, and benefit-first. Follow the Imgix brand voice for all copy.

## Email Types

### Customer Announcement Email
Sent to existing Imgix customers when a feature launches.

**Subject line:** Short, specific, benefit-led.
- Good: "New: Turn any image into video with one URL"
- Good: "Your images just got faster. Here's what changed"
- Bad: "Important update regarding your Imgix account"
- Bad: "YOU WON'T BELIEVE What We Just Launched!!!"

**Body structure:**
1. One sentence: what's new and why it matters to the reader
2. 2-3 sentences: the key capabilities or benefits (use bullets if listing 3+ benefits)
3. One clear CTA: "See what's new" or "Try it now" (with UTM parameters)
4. Sign off: "The Imgix team"

**Total length:** 3-5 sentences. The reader should scan the entire email in under 30 seconds. If it feels too short, it's probably right.

### Prospect/Nurture Email
Sent to prospects or leads who aren't yet customers.

**Subject line:** Lead with the problem the feature solves or the outcome it enables.
- Good: "Stop managing five tools for one visual workflow"
- Good: "Video optimization without the infrastructure headache"

**Body structure:**
1. One sentence: the pain point or opportunity
2. 1-2 sentences: how Imgix addresses it (tied to the new feature)
3. One CTA: "Start your free trial" or "See how it works" (with UTM parameters)

**Total length:** Even shorter than customer emails. 2-4 sentences.

## General Email Rules

- One primary CTA per email. Don't dilute with multiple asks.
- Paragraphs: 1-2 sentences for mobile readability
- No filler adverbs, no em dash overuse
- All CTA links must include UTMs: `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`
- Campaign: `utm_campaign=q[quarter]_[year]_[feature]_launch`
- Content tag: `utm_content=cta_primary` or `utm_content=cta_secondary`

## A/B Testing

For major launches, draft 2 subject line variants:
- Variant A: Benefit-led (what the reader gets)
- Variant B: Feature-led (what's new)

Example:
- A: "Optimize and deliver video with the same simplicity as images"
- B: "New: Imgix Video now supports HLS, DASH, and closed captions"
