# Social Media Guide for Product Launches

All social posts follow Imgix brand voice. Slightly more energetic than blog posts, but still Imgix: confident, clear, no hype.

## LinkedIn

This is Imgix's primary social channel for launches.

**Voice:** Write like a smart person at the company sharing something they find genuinely interesting. Not a brand pushing a product.

**Structure:**
1. Lead with an insight, observation, or data point (not a pitch)
2. 1-2 sentences on what's new and why it matters
3. Close with a link to the blog post or landing page (with UTMs)

**Length:** 3-5 sentences total. Say the one thing that matters and link out.

**Example:**
> For every second of loading time saved, conversion rates increase by 10%. That's why we built Imgix Video to optimize and deliver video with the same URL-based simplicity you already use for images. Our latest release adds HLS streaming, closed captions, and smart video cropping. One platform, every visual asset. [Link]

**What to avoid:**
- Staccato fragment patterns ("No X. No Y. No Z.")
- Ad copy that reads like a landing page
- Hashtag stuffing
- Emoji overload (one per post is fine when it adds clarity)

**UTMs:** `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic_social`

## X / Twitter

**Voice:** Tight, punchy, benefit-first. Link to longer content for the full story.

**Structure:** One or two sentences + link. That's it.

**Example:**
> New: Auto-generate video previews from any video with a single URL parameter. No transcoding pipeline required. [Link]

**UTMs:** `utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=organic_social`

## Instagram

Adapt the LinkedIn post for visual-first presentation. Focus on the visual (screenshot, GIF, short video clip) and keep caption concise.

**UTMs:** `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social`

## Bluesky

Similar to X/Twitter in length and tone. Slightly more conversational if the audience skews that way.

**UTMs:** `utm_source=bluesky&utm_medium=organic_social`

## Posting Cadence for Launches

For a standard launch, plan 2-3 posts across platforms:
1. **Launch day:** Primary announcement post on LinkedIn and X
2. **Day 2-3:** Follow-up post with a specific use case or customer angle
3. **Week 2:** Deeper dive or "here's what people are building" post

For major launches, extend to 4-5 posts and consider paid amplification.
