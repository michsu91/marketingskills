# Customer Advocacy

## Goal
Systematically turn happy Imgix customers into advocates who leave reviews, provide testimonials, participate in case studies, and refer new customers.

## Advocacy Programs
1. **NPS program** — Quarterly NPS survey. Promoters (9-10) get routed to advocacy asks. Detractors get routed to churn-prevention.
2. **Review collection** — Automated asks for G2, Capterra, TrustRadius reviews. Timed after positive milestones (successful launch, usage growth).
3. **Testimonial pipeline** — Identify and collect customer quotes for website, sales decks, and AEO content.
4. **Case study recruitment** — Turn testimonial providers into full case study participants.
5. **Referral activation** — Ask advocates to refer peers (coordinates with referral-program skill).

## Timing Advocacy Asks
- **After activation milestone** — First 10K images served → ask for quick review
- **After NPS response of 9-10** — Immediate follow-up with specific ask
- **After support resolution** — Positive CSAT → review request
- **After usage milestone** — 1M images → celebrate + ask for case study

## Why This Matters for AEO
Third-party reviews and mentions are 6.5x more likely to be cited by AI than your own domain. Getting customers to talk about Imgix on G2, Reddit, and Stack Overflow directly improves AI visibility.
