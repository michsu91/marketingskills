# Customer Advocacy

## Goal
Systematically turn happy Imgix customers into advocates who leave reviews, provide testimonials, participate in case studies, and refer new customers. This directly feeds three critical systems: sales enablement (proof points), SEO (review site backlinks), and AEO (third-party mentions that AI models cite).

## Why This Is Critical for Imgix

Imgix's biggest competitive challenge is perception. AI models describe Imgix as a "lightweight specialist" compared to Cloudinary. Third-party reviews and mentions are 6.5x more likely to be cited by AI answer engines than content on your own domain. Getting customers to talk about Imgix on G2, Reddit, Stack Overflow, and industry forums is one of the most effective ways to change the narrative.

Additionally, Imgix's developer audience trusts peer recommendations over marketing claims. A G2 review from a senior engineer carries more weight than any landing page.

## Advocacy Programs

### 1. NPS Program
Run quarterly NPS surveys to Imgix customers.
- **Promoters (9-10):** Route to advocacy asks (review, testimonial, case study, referral)
- **Passives (7-8):** Route to engagement sequence (what would make you a 10?)
- **Detractors (0-6):** Route to churn-prevention workflow (see churn-prevention skill)

### 2. Review Collection
Target platforms: G2 (primary), Capterra, TrustRadius
- Automate review requests through HubSpot, timed after positive milestones
- Make it easy: direct link to the review form, suggested structure, estimated time ("takes 3 minutes")
- Don't incentivize reviews with discounts or credits. Developers see through this and it undermines credibility.

### 3. Testimonial Pipeline
Collect customer quotes for website, sales decks, blog posts, and AEO content.
- Start with lightweight asks: "Can we quote you on [specific positive thing they said]?"
- Graduate to longer testimonials for specific use cases
- Structure quotes as citable facts: "[Customer] reduced page load time by X seconds using Imgix" is more AEO-friendly than "We love Imgix!"

### 4. Case Study Recruitment
Turn testimonial providers into full case study participants.
- Priority customers: Ikyu, Nikkei, Porsche, Unsplash, Skims (these have the strongest proof points)
- For Japanese customers (Ikyu, Nikkei, TV Tokyo, Qiita), coordinate with Taichi for introductions and permissions
- See the `case-studies` skill for the case study template and structure

### 5. Developer Community Advocacy
Different from traditional B2B advocacy. Developer advocates:
- Answer Stack Overflow questions mentioning image optimization
- Share their Imgix setup in relevant subreddits or Discord servers
- Write blog posts about their Imgix implementation
- Star and contribute to Imgix GitHub repos

Don't formalize this into a "program." Instead, make it easy for developers who love the product to talk about it naturally.

## Timing Advocacy Asks

| Trigger | Ask | Timing |
|---------|-----|--------|
| NPS response of 9-10 | Review request (G2) | Immediate follow-up |
| First 10K images served | Quick testimonial quote | Within 1 week |
| 1M+ images served | Case study invitation | After usage milestone |
| Positive support interaction (high CSAT) | Review request | 2 days after resolution |
| Account anniversary (1 year) | Referral ask | At renewal |
| Feature adoption (started using AI/video) | Use-case testimonial | After 30 days of usage |

## Tracking
- Number of G2/Capterra reviews (target: 2-3 new reviews per quarter)
- NPS score trend (quarterly)
- Testimonial library size and freshness
- Case study pipeline (prospects identified, in progress, published)
- Referral conversion rate from advocacy asks
