# Expansion & Upsell

## Goal
Increase net revenue retention by proactively identifying accounts ready to grow and nudging them at the right moment. Imgix's usage-based pricing means expansion should happen naturally — this skill makes it intentional.

## Expansion Triggers
1. **Usage threshold** — Account hits 80% of plan limit → automated upgrade nudge
2. **Feature discovery** — Account starts using advanced features (AI transforms, video) → upsell to higher tier
3. **Traffic spike** — Seasonal or growth-driven traffic increase → proactive capacity conversation
4. **New use case** — Account connects additional storage sources → cross-sell opportunity
5. **Team growth** — Additional team members added → potential enterprise tier

## Messaging Principles
- Frame upgrades as "you're growing, here's how to keep up" not "you're hitting limits"
- Show value delivered before asking for more: "You've served 2M optimized images this month"
- Make upgrading self-serve and frictionless (PLG)
- For larger accounts, trigger sales assist at the right moment
